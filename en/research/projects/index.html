Projects

This is neither a showcase of genres nor a list of platforms.

It is a map of Ashraellen’s working fields, distinguishing what is being researched now from artistic models, public practice, and side laboratories.

The upper layer of the map contains four current research lines. They differ in subject and method, but meet around one question: what happens to human agency, perception, memory, voice and the capacity to choose when an internal or external intermediary appears between the person and reality.

Current research lines

SEVEN

A research-through-fiction cycle around seven historically persistent categories of human vulnerability: pride, greed, lust, envy, gluttony, wrath and sloth/acedia.

SEVEN does not treat this seven-part scheme as established truth. It tests whether the mechanisms can be distinguished consistently, where they begin to overlap, and whether the classification survives explicit attempts to falsify it.

Seven human vulnerabilities — or seven old names for something more complex?

VOICEPRINT

An artistic-research project on authorship, language and AI-mediated transformation of text.

VOICEPRINT asks what must remain intact through translation, adaptation and machine transformation so that the author does not disappear together with the words — their rhythm, position, tone and way of seeing.

At what point does assistance stop serving the author and begin to replace them?

Open VOICEPRINT →

MY MEMORY

Research into long-term memory in human work with AI: how decisions, reasons, sources and project state can remain recoverable across sessions, models and providers.

The central question is not only technical. Who controls a person’s durable context, what counts as their memory, and how can continuity be preserved without surrendering it to a single system?

Your memory should belong to you.

Open MY MEMORY →

WHISPER

Research into live AI-mediated communication between people who do not share a common language.

WHISPER tests whether a machine can help a person speak through an unfamiliar language while preserving their authorship of the conversation, their tone, intention and responsibility for what is said.

If the machine supplies the language, at what point does assistance become authorship?

Artistic model

MONOLITH

Ashraellen’s main long-term artistic-research model.

The trilogy examines how a person loses separateness, memory, language, body and inner ground inside a system that first promises order and then turns the living into material.

BETON, SLUDGE and GAS are three states of reality: solidification, decay and the dispersion of meaning.

The novel works here not as an escape story, but as a model through which a mechanism can be lived and seen from within.

Satirical field

Church of Saint Whine

A satirical laboratory of exhaustion, poverty, self-deception and sacred whining.

The project uses pseudo-religious language, absurdity and irony to show how a person can turn powerlessness into a cult and complaint into a way of not moving.

A pseudo-sacred text about the ways people justify powerlessness, exhaustion, poverty, procrastination and inner surrender.

This map shows different levels of one practice. SEVEN, VOICEPRINT, MY MEMORY and WHISPER are current research lines. MONOLITH is a long-term artistic model. Public channels bring ideas into living response. Satire and side laboratories test related questions through irony, sound, image, symbol, taste and value.

What joins them is not one genre or one technology, but an attempt to see the mechanisms through which a person loses or regains clarity, authorship, memory, discernment and their own position in relation to reality.
