Projects

This page gathers the major research directions of Ashraellen: books, channels, cycles, artistic-philosophical models, and public formats through which observation takes form.

This is not a showcase of genres. It is a map of working instruments: some unfold a mechanism in the depth of a novel, others capture a quick cross-section through video, posts, sound, satire, symbol, or the reader’s response.

central field / clarity and perception

Ashraellen

The main channel on clarity, awareness, inner silence, and the human return to oneself. It studies the moment when the mind stops making noise and understanding arrives not as theory, but as lived experience.

Here, one speaks not from oneself, but through oneself. The question: how does a person begin to see again after mistaking reactions for reality?

novel laboratory / social matter

MONOLITH

The trilogy studies three states of human and social reality: solidification, liquefaction, and evaporation of meaning. It is a model of how a system enters a person through language, fear, order, habit, and voluntary consent.

BETON — solidification. SLUDGE — the collapse of form. GAS — the invisible field of influence.

digital perception / confirmation of reality

You Are Already Online

The novel studies the human condition inside a digital environment, where reality is increasingly perceived not directly, but through signals, interfaces, expectations, and the inner readiness to confirm what is happening.

The question is not whether a person is connected. The question is when they stopped noticing the connection.

satirical philosophy / burnout and attention

Church of Saint Whine

A project about burnout, the culture of endless striving, toxic positivity, and the honest language of exhaustion. Antimotivation and ironic therapy become a way to return the right to calm without spiritual gloss.

Here, whining is not surrender, but a liturgy of honesty: every sigh becomes a small medicine.

symbols / myths / hidden cultural codes

Occultus Scriptorium

The channel deciphers hidden meanings in sacred texts, myths, fairy tales, sayings, films, and pop culture. It looks for recurring patterns between ancient parables and modern images.

Text is only a mask. The true meaning is almost always hidden deeper, between the lines.

money / value / living balance

Ordo Sacrunomiconis

A philosophy of finance and a sacred inquiry into money, wealth, and value. Money is treated as movement, profit as perception, and abundance as attunement with flow.

This is not a course and not a religion, but a middle path between economy and awakening, the markets of matter and the silence of meaning.

sound / meditation / inner environment

Meditation Melodies & Sounds

A sound space for peace, renewal, and deep relaxation. Music is studied not as decoration, but as a path toward inner balance, concentration, and a gentle return to oneself.

Sometimes meaning comes not through words, but through the silence between sounds.

posts / quick formulas / visual fragments

Instagram

A field of short records: visual formulas, fragments of observation, images, and brief texts in which thought has not yet become a treatise, but has already stopped being accidental.

A post is not a broken-off thought. Sometimes it is a seed from which a whole system grows.

Russian channel / flow of observations

Telegram

A Russian-language channel for live observations, working formulations, short messages, and thoughts that have not yet become a book, a video, or a finished text.

Telegram is not an archive of final truths, but a place where thought is still warm.

satirical apocrypha / book as ritual

The Book of Whinesis

A pseudo-sacred satirical form studying burnout, digital salvation, the cult of busyness, notification addiction, and the human ability to turn exhaustion into an almost religion.

Whining here is not weakness, but a symptom of an age that is tired and still keeps updating.

field observations / reactions and comments

Public Field

Short videos, posts, and comments work as fast research sensors. Here, thought meets the living human response: agreement, resistance, irritation, gratitude, misunderstanding, and sudden recognition.

The public format shows where thought truly touches a person, and where it remains only a beautiful phrase.
