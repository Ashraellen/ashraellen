Projects

This is not a showcase of genres and not a list of platforms.

It is a map of Ashraellen’s working fields.

Some projects are central research models. Others are public zones where thought meets living reaction. Others work as side laboratories: through satire, sound, taste, image, symbol, and everyday perception.

Central field

MONOLITH

The main artistic-research model of Ashraellen.

The trilogy studies how a person loses separateness, memory, language, body, and inner ground inside a system that first promises order and then turns the living into material.

BETON, SLUDGE, and GAS are three states of reality: solidification, decay, and the dispersion of meaning.

The novel works here not as an escape story, but as a model through which a mechanism can be seen from within.

Satirical field

Church of Saint Whine

A satirical laboratory of fatigue, poverty, self-deception, and sacred whining.

The project uses pseudo-religious language, absurdity, and irony to show how a person turns their own helplessness into a cult, and complaint into a way not to move.

Open YouTube →Sanctuary →

The Book of Whinesis

The textual core of the satirical canon of Church of Saint Whine.

It is not a motivational book in reverse, but an anti-manual: a pseudo-sacred text about how a person justifies helplessness, fatigue, poverty, procrastination, and inner surrender.

Satire becomes a diagnostic instrument here: complaint exposes the place where a person has mistaken exhaustion for identity.

Side laboratories

Occultus Scriptorium

A laboratory of hidden meanings, cultural images, symbols, myths, and stories.

It studies not “secret knowledge” as decoration, but the way culture speaks to a person through images, fears, archetypes, and recurring forms.

Melodies & Sounds of Inner Spirit

The sound direction of Ashraellen.

Music, sounds, and meditative forms work here not as background for relaxation, but as a space where attention returns to itself without unnecessary explanations.

Open YouTube →

Sacrunomicon

A philosophy of finance and living balance.

The project studies money, wealth, and value as movement, exchange, and perception.

Here money is movement. Profit is perception. Abundance is attunement with flow.

It is not a course and not a religion, but a space between economy and inner clarity: an attempt to see how value appears, disappears, returns, and reflects the state of a person.

Open →

Sacred Taste

Kitchen as meditation.

The project studies how a simple action returns a person to presence: cutting, mixing, waiting, tasting, feeling smell, warmth, taste, and the silence of the process.

Food does not become a cult or an ornament of life. It remains direct contact with matter, body, and the moment.

All these directions differ in form, but are bound by one task: to record the mechanisms through which a person loses clarity, and to create forms in which these mechanisms become visible.

MONOLITH shows the mechanism deeply and from within. The main Ashraellen channel speaks it directly. Social networks test thought in living reaction. Satire opens self-deception through laughter. Side laboratories work with perception, image, sound, taste, symbol, and value.

These are not different projects for the sake of quantity. They are different entrances into one research.
