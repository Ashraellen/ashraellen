Research

This is where research, observation, and formulation are recorded — as a protocol of experience. The central question: where interpretation ends and reality begins.

Ashraellen Research is a space of artistic-philosophical inquiry into perception, language, power, environment, and the inner transformation of the human being. This work does not exist only in books. Novels, essays, short videos, posts, comments, and visual images are different forms of one investigation.

Books create deep models of reality. Short videos and posts compress an observation into a formula, an image, or a precise strike of meaning. Social networks become the field part of the research, where a thought meets a living human reaction: agreement, resistance, irritation, gratitude, misunderstanding, and sudden recognition.

  • Books — deep models and research instruments.
  • Videos — quick cross-sections of perception, fear, language, and inner turning.
  • Posts — formulas of observation, short records, and fragments of meaning.
  • Comments — field testing of thought in contact with a living person.
  • The website — an archive, a map, and a point of assembly for the whole inquiry.

I do not write philosophical treatises in the usual form. I create forms through which an idea can not only be understood, but also experienced: seen at work inside oneself, revealing mechanisms that usually remain invisible.

I write novels as research instruments, and short texts and videos as fast sensors. Not to entertain the reader or viewer, but to make visible the processes that an ordinary philosophical text can only describe.
