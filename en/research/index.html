Research

I record the mechanisms through which the human being of the twenty-first century loses clarity, inner ground, and contact with reality.

This is not a system of help.

No one is healed here, led here, or promised a way out.

The central question:

where interpretation ends
and reality begins.

What begins here is not instruction, but discernment.

What is being researched

Not a separate anxiety.

Not a separate dependency.

Not a separate error of perception.

But the whole mechanism:

how a person loses inner ground,
begins to mistake reaction for event,
lives inside other people’s scripts,
and functions where presence should have been.

Information noise, the power of language and interpretation, the digital environment as a substitute for living perception, fear of the future, loss of meaning, and existential burnout are not a list of symptoms.

They are a map of the territory.

How it is done

I write novels as research instruments.

Short texts and videos act as fast sensors.

Books, essays, videos, publications, comments, and visual images are different ways of carrying out the same work.

Social networks are its field layer: a thought meets living reaction, resistance, irritation, gratitude, and sudden recognition.

Method

The method of this work is observation.

Not an external system, not an academic procedure, and not a set of ready-made theses, but an attentive discernment of how explanations, reactions, impulses, and attempts to name what is happening arise within consciousness.

For more on why literary form, first-person observation, and the reader’s response become part of the research, see “Method of Observation”.

Forms of research

I do not explain ideas — I create forms through which a person sees their action within themselves.

This is a fundamental difference.

A philosophical text describes a mechanism.

A novel allows it to be lived from within.

A short post or video strikes precisely at the moment when the mechanism is already working — and the person notices it.

I do not offer salvation from outside.

What matters here is not a ready-made formula, but the possibility of discerning the mechanism itself.

What a person does with what has been seen remains their own responsibility.
