About the author

Ashraellen is the working name of Nikolai Kostyshev, an independent writer and artistic researcher living in Poland.

The author’s work brings together literary prose, phenomenological observation, social satire, digital culture and public philosophical practice. Through books, essays, videos, publications and a multilingual archive, the project shows how a contemporary person loses clarity, inner support and contact with reality — and how these processes can be made visible through form.

Russian is the primary language of thought and writing for Nikolai Kostyshev (Ashraellen): the language in which the original meanings, rhythm and inner pressure of the work arise. Other language versions are created as careful literary adaptations and channels of access to the original core.

The project does not offer a system of help or spiritual instruction. It creates forms of observation: novels, formulas, essays, images, videos and public reflections through which the reader or viewer can recognise structures already active within experience.

Position of observation

Ashraellen studies how the person of the twenty-first century loses cognitive and existential orientation: how interpretation replaces direct perception, how external narratives substitute subjectivity, how informational noise reshapes attention and how presence gradually gives way to functioning.

The central question of this practice is simple and difficult: where does interpretation end, and where does reality begin?

The material develops through literary projects, short public texts, videos, comments, reader response and multilingual publication. Social networks become a field part of the work: thought meets resistance, irritation, recognition, gratitude and dispute.

Method of observation

The approach is based on phenomenological observation: careful tracking of structures of consciousness from within, without reducing them too quickly to external categories.

Artistic form is not decoration here. It becomes a way of knowing. The novel works as a long optical instrument; essays and formulas record observations at the moment of clarity; video and social networks test thought in live public response; visual images address pre-conceptual structures of perception.

The universality of observation is tested not only by statistics, but by recognition: the moment when the reader encounters a description of their own experience where they did not expect to find it.

Professional frames

Ashraellen can be understood through several complementary professional frames. These frames do not divide the project into separate directions; they clarify how one practice can be read by different cultural, research and digital contexts.

Artistic research

A practice-based inquiry where novels, essays, video, sound, images and public response function as instruments of observation. Artistic form is treated as a way of investigating experience, not as decoration around a thesis.

Open knowledge

A multilingual public form of philosophical reflection outside closed academic or institutional spaces. The project creates accessible points of entry without simplifying the original density of the work.

Digital society

An observation of how digital speed, information noise, algorithmic pressure and fragmented attention affect meaning, identity, inner ground and contact with reality.

Independent digital archive

A structured public archive that preserves texts, books, observations, fragments and research directions outside the disappearing flow of social media platforms.

Current project seeking support

The current stage of Ashraellen requires sustained working time, technical resources and editorial concentration in order to turn dispersed materials into a coherent multilingual artistic-research platform.

The immediate need is a twelve-month period of stabilised work: time for writing, systematisation, publication, translation, archive development, video and audio production, and the strengthening of the digital infrastructure already built around Ashraellen.com.

The project is seeking support for the development of Ashraellen.com, the systematisation of the public archive, the preparation of books, the translation and adaptation of materials, the production of video and audio formats, and the creation of a clear public structure for readers, viewers, publishers, research partners and cultural institutions.

Support is needed not for expansion for its own sake, but for the transition from fragmented work to a stable cycle: observation → text → publication → translation → public response → archive.

Development plan

The next stage of the project is connected with shaping existing materials into a stable working system and continuing those directions that reveal the research in different public registers.

  • create new philosophical-research videos for the main Ashraellen channel;
  • gradually translate key videos into the project’s multilingual format;
  • fully rework part of the early videos, preserving their core while bringing the form to the current precision of understanding;
  • continue the video and audio format of Whinesis, including the next chapters of the book;
  • bring back short satirical formats from the characters of “Church” of Saint Whine;
  • continue symbolic analyses of fairy tales, cartoons, films and cultural plots on Occultus Scriptorium;
  • develop the sound direction of inner peace and presence;
  • create the first materials for Sacred Taste as a project about cooking, attention and everyday ritual;
  • complete the third volume of the MONOLITH trilogy — GAS;
  • prepare The Book of Whinesis / Книгу Нытия for publication at least in electronic form;
  • continue publishing daily formulas of observation on Instagram and Telegram;
  • develop Ashraellen.com as a multilingual archive of books, observations, public materials and the professional dossier.

Technical infrastructure

The project has already moved beyond simple text work. It now includes video editing, multilingual voiceover, image preparation, processing of large archives, website development, digital publication of books and work with several language versions.

This requires reliable production infrastructure: computing power, storage and backup of materials, peripherals for stable editorial and editing work, as well as professional tools for video, audio, translation, design and publication.

In this context, equipment and subscriptions are not personal luxury. They are working means of the project, without which the speed and quality of production become dependent on technical limitations rather than on the research task.

Expected outcomes

Support will make it possible not merely to continue the work, but to gather it into measurable public results.

As a result, Ashraellen.com will be developed as a multilingual platform with sections for books, observations, public materials, a professional dossier, contact and legal transparency. The public archive will be cleaned, selected and structured so that the reader can enter it not through the chaos of publications, but through clear sections: “Formulas”, “Mini-essays”, “Fragments” and “Sources”.

A separate outcome will be the further preparation of books and literary projects as forms of artistic knowledge, as well as the translation and adaptation of key materials into English, Polish and other languages. This will allow the project to move beyond the Russian-speaking context without losing the original meaning and density.

New videos, voiceovers, subtitles, short formats and materials will also be created for an audience that perceives thought through voice, rhythm and visual form.

The result will not be merely a set of publications, but a sustainable working cycle: observation, text, publication, translation, archiving and public feedback.

What the audience will receive

The audience will receive access to precise, non-simplified forms of observation that help recognise mechanisms of anxiety, self-deception, loss of inner support and rupture with reality — without motivational rhetoric, ideological frames or ready-made recipes.

The materials will be available in the nine languages of the main group and presented in different formats and at different levels of depth:

  • short public formulas for quick recognition;
  • mini-essays for calm reflection;
  • fragments and large literary works for deep immersion;
  • video and audio materials for those who perceive thought better through voice and rhythm.

This allows each person to enter the project with the degree of involvement and in the language that corresponds to their current state, attention and inner readiness.

Public response

The project already generates a living response. Individual videos and lectures, in particular on discipline of mind and the stopping of inner noise, reach tens and hundreds of thousands of views. One video on the main channel has exceeded half a million views and gathered many comments. Audience reactions — resistance, recognition, gratitude, dispute — themselves become important material for this work.

Cooperation and support

The project is open to publishing cooperation, cultural programmes, residencies, grant support, institutional partnership and private patronage.

Support will make it possible to accelerate and stabilise the work: to free sustained working time, complete major texts, prepare publications, develop the multilingual archive, produce video and audio materials in a regular rhythm and preserve quality without the constant rupture between creative work and external employment.

PrincipleThe aim is to make the work more accessible to enter, not easier to dilute. Support should help create clearer points of access, translations, publications and public formats while preserving the pressure, precision and density of the original research.
