What Happens After Death?

An exploration of the fear of death through simple metaphors: the ocean, the cinema, virtual reality and a playlist. The video examines the possibility that death, in the usual sense, is not the end of consciousness, but the dissolution of the form with which we mistakenly identify ourselves.

This is not a new belief and not a promise of an afterlife scenario. It is an attempt to look beyond familiar ideas about the body, personality, time and the “expiration date” of a human story.

The video contains multilingual audio tracks. The language can be changed in the YouTube player settings.

Seven Levels of Self-Deception

A radical inventory of the familiar ways in which a person deceives themselves: from the phrase “I’m fine” to endless self-improvement, expectations, escape and the confusion of thoughts with life itself.

This is not motivation and not a list of mistakes. It is an observation of how a person turns into a reaction, postpones life until later and tries to control themselves where they first need to honestly see what is happening.

The video contains multilingual audio tracks. The language can be changed in the YouTube player settings.

Your Moan Is Sacred | Revelation IV

Revelation IV from the “Church” of Saint Whine: a satirical and philosophical analysis of complaint as humanity’s oldest language, the first form of self-protection and a step toward awareness.

This is not a call to remain stuck in whining, but an ironic restoration of the right to say: “I am uncomfortable.” Where complaint stops being weakness, it may become the beginning of discernment and change.

Saint Whine: sacred irony, existential humor and the philosophy of human complaint.

Pinocchio: Chronicle of a Dead Soul

An analysis of the dark reality hidden in Carlo Collodi’s original story: not a sweet children’s tale, but a disturbing guide to how a wooden puppet becomes a human being.

The video examines biblical parallels, the connection between Geppetto and the image of the creator, the Fox and the Cat as modern fake gurus and success coaches, and metanoia — the alchemy of turning wood into soul.

A video from the Scriptorium line: decoding hidden meanings, archetypes and the dark foundations of familiar stories.

Money & … / The Sacred Book of AHEPSU

The first page of the Sacred Book of AHEPSU — an entrance into a space where money stops being an external topic and becomes a mirror of the person standing beside it.

This is not a talk about the secrets of wealth and not a promise of an easy path. It is a hard and honest encounter with greed, hope, fear, self-deception and the inner place from which the conversation about money truly begins.

You Know Too Much to Get Rich | Page II

The second page of the Sacred Book of AHEPSU, about how knowledge, methods, laws of success and the endless gathering of information can become not a path to wealth, but a convenient justification for inaction.

This is an analysis of the point of descent: the moment when knowledge stops working, comfort becomes a quiet cage, and clarity begins only after the refusal of familiar excuses.

Sacrunomicon / AHEPSU: money, comfort, self-deception and the beginning of clarity.

Sacred Taste — Journey into Silence

The introductory video of the Sacred Taste channel — a direction of Ashraellen where a woman slowly cooks food while a calm voice accompanies the preparation with reflections on attention, inner harmony and sacred taste.

These are not simply recipes, but the kitchen as meditation: plant-based dishes, the ritual of tea, care for oneself, gentle observations and the feeling that every ingredient and every gesture can become a step toward a more conscious life.

Introductory video of the Sacred Taste channel: slow cooking, a female voice, food as meditation and the kitchen as a space of presence.
