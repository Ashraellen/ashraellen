What Happens After Death?

A reflection on the fear of death through simple metaphors: the ocean, the cinema, virtual reality, and the playlist. The video explores the possibility that death, as we usually understand it, is not the end of consciousness, but the dissolution of a form we mistakenly identify with.

This is not a new belief and not a promise of an afterlife scenario. It is an attempt to look beyond habitual ideas about the body, personality, time, and the “expiration date” of a human story.

Seven Levels of Self-Deception

A radical inventory of the familiar ways a person deceives themselves: from “I’m fine” to endless self-improvement, expectations, escape, and confusing thoughts about life with life itself.

This is not motivation and not a list of mistakes. It is an observation of how a person becomes a reaction, postpones life, and tries to control themselves where the first step is to honestly see what is happening.

Money & … / The Sacred Book of AHEPSU

The first page of the Sacred Book of AHEPSU — an entrance into a space where money stops being an external topic and becomes a mirror of the person standing next to it.

This is not a talk about secrets of wealth and not a promise of an easy path. It is a hard and honest encounter with greed, hope, fear, self-deception, and the inner place where the real conversation about money begins.
