Public Formula 06

A Deeper Gaze Gathers Life

When a person begins to see deeper than the visible, they stop wandering among consequences and begin to see causes.

Meaning

Deep understanding does not make life simple, but it makes it more connected. A person stops perceiving their mistakes and suffering as meaningless chaos and begins to see in them the chain that led to inner understanding.

Full text

When a person begins to see deeper than the visible, they stand more firmly in life.

Then they no longer wander among consequences, no longer gather fragments with the face of a great martyr, and no longer ask the Universe why it has once again chosen them, so beautiful.

They begin to see causes. And that means understanding that life is not obliged to be simple, but it is almost always more logical than it seems in a moment of hysteria.

Without such a gaze, a person lives like a blind one in a labyrinth: bumping into walls, taking offence at corners and suspecting the furniture of conspiracy.

Insight begins where you stop looking only outward...

And suddenly life appears in such a cross-section that all your mistakes, failures and suffering turn out to be an absolutely necessary chain leading to the thought, to the understanding of time, that you have gained within yourself.

And you realize with horror that you would have understood nothing without these sufferings, without these failures, without this pain.

Lord, how precisely everything came together.

Accept as it is what you cannot change, and may joy be with you...

Why it was chosen

This post completes an important Ashraellen line: the human path begins with pain, disappointment and collision with one’s own illusions, but it does not end there. True maturity comes when a person begins to see not only their suffering, but also its semantic necessity.

Research note

Insight here is not a mystical illumination and not a beautiful spiritual state. It is the ability to see the causality of one’s own life deeper than habitual complaint. The post shows the transition from reaction to understanding, from the labyrinth of consequences to the inner logic of lived experience.
