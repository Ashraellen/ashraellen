Public

Here the research leaves books, drafts, and inner formulas and enters the living field: videos, publications, comments, replies, reactions, and open observations.

The public field is not a display case of activity. It is the place where thought is tested through living contact with a person. Here it is no longer protected by the form of a book: it becomes a short phrase, an image, a video, a reply, irritation, gratitude, or sudden recognition.

If books show the mechanism in depth, public form records its flashes: where a thought was accepted, where it provoked resistance, where it was distorted, and where it suddenly became recognizable.

Selected Public Formulas

Six publications chosen as the first reference points of the Ashraellen public field. They are not just social-media posts, but compact research instruments: each records a way of seeing the person, fear, awakening, finitude, power, or the meaning of lived experience.

Public Formula 01

Cheerfulness as a Diagnosis of a Person

Laughter often reveals a person more precisely than correct speeches. It shows whether they can rejoice without poison, without humiliation, and without inner malice.

If you want to understand who is before you, look at how they laugh.
