Public

Here the research leaves books, drafts and inner formulas and enters the living field: videos, publications, comments, replies, reactions and open observations.

The public field is not a display case of activity. It is the place where thought is tested through living contact with a person. Here it is no longer protected by the form of a book: it becomes a short phrase, an image, a video, a reply, irritation, gratitude or sudden recognition.

If books show the mechanism in depth, public form records its flashes: where a thought was accepted, where it caused resistance, where it was distorted, and where it suddenly became recognizable.

Selected Public Formulas

Six publications chosen as the first reference points of the Ashraellen public field. They are not just social-media posts, but short research instruments: each one records a separate way to see the person, fear, awakening, finitude, power or the meaning of what has been lived.

Formula 01

Cheerfulness as a Diagnosis of a Person

Laughter often opens a person more precisely than their correct speeches. It shows whether they can rejoice without poison, without the desire to humiliate, and without inner malice.

A person is visible not only in words, but also in how they laugh.

Formula 02

The Same Forces, New Names

Modern anxieties, addictions and neuroses are considered as new names for old gods and demons. A person may change the language, but does not always become free from service.

Demons have not disappeared. They have simply received modern names.

Open formula →

Formula 03

Awakening Begins When Continuing Becomes Impossible

An inner turning often begins not with a ready plan, but with a clear feeling: it cannot go on like this. Awakening here is not a status, but an honest letting go of the illusion about oneself.

You cannot prepare for the jump into yourself.

Open formula →

Formula 04

Finitude Awakens the Question

Philosophy often begins not from satiety, but from collision with death, pain and limit. Real thought comes when former illusions no longer hold.

Many begin to think when their illusions run out.

Open formula →

Formula 05

Fear as a Mechanism of Control

Where a person stops fearing and begins to think independently, the power of systems built on prohibition, dependence and a prescribed scenario weakens.

An independent person is harder to control.

Open formula →

Formula 06

A Deeper Gaze Gathers Life

When a person begins to see causes, mistakes and suffering stop being chaos. They may turn out to be the necessary chain leading to the understanding that has matured within them.

Insight begins where the gaze stops being directed only outward.
