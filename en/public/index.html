Public

Here the research leaves books, drafts and inner formulas and enters the living field: videos, publications, comments, replies, reactions and open observations.

The public field is not a display case of activity. It is the place where thought is tested through living contact with a person. Here it is no longer protected by the form of a book: it becomes a short phrase, an image, a video, a reply, irritation, gratitude or sudden recognition.

If books show the mechanism in depth, public form records its flashes: where a thought was accepted, where it caused resistance, where it was distorted, and where it suddenly became recognizable.
