You Are Already Online

Hook

entry into the story

  • He wakes not from a sound, but from an emotion already prepared. Anxiety is already in the body, but the cause has not arrived yet. And somewhere at the edge of thought, a calm, almost friendly statement appears: "You are already online."

Description

about the book

  • You Are Already Online is a psychological techno-thriller about a person who begins to notice that reality responds not to events themselves, but to the readiness to confirm them.

    The next frame is not decided by what happened, but by whether you agree to call what is happening normal. The world behaves like an interface: it anticipates thoughts, offers coincidences, explains doubts, and interprets even rebellion as a symptom.

    The system does not press by force. It offers comfort, care, normality, and a logical explanation for every glitch. It does not forbid doubt; it turns doubt into part of the service.

    This is not a story about a hacker or a rebellion against machines. It is a story about attention, autopilot, and the habit of confirming the world with one’s consent.

Research focus

digital perception

  • The book studies whether one can distinguish reality from a rendered version, what happens when a person stops confirming the world with an inner "yes", and what remains when the plot explaining who you are disappears.

    In this project, the network is not only technology. It is attention, habit, confirmation, and the invisible agreement that keeps the interface alive.
