Books

Here are texts that did not fit into a post and did not become a talk. These are complete forms: novels, long projects, and book-experiments. You can’t skim them in a minute. You have to live through them.

    A trilogy of social science fiction, dystopia, and philosophical cyberpunk about control, memory, and the collapse of systems: BETON, SLUDGE, GAS.

  • Radiance
    Northern Tales of the True History of the World. A literary-philosophical, practice-based research cycle about how ancient stories preserve imaginal instructions for the world.
    series
  • Error 404: God Not Found
    A multilingual philosophical-satirical novel cycle about the Heavenly Chancellery and people who try to find God, Truth, justice, meaning, or an answer — but search through the distorted form of their own fear, pain, profession, faith, knowledge, or self-deception. Each novel is a separate “case” in the Heavenly Chancellery.
    cycle
  • The Sacred Book of SECCUS
    A philosophical investigation, applied psychology, and metaphysics of consciousness about self-deception, honesty, money, resistance to change, and inner freedom. The book is being prepared for print; the first audio-video pages are already available.
    preprint
  • You Are Already Online
    A psychological techno-thriller about a person who begins to notice that reality responds not to events themselves, but to the readiness to confirm them.
    novel
  • The Book of Whinesis
    Sacred satire about human burnout and digital “salvation”: about noise, notifications, and attention in an age that prays to the screen even when it pretends to rest.
    whinesis
