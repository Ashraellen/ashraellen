Privacy Policy

This page explains how Ashraellen.com may collect, use and protect personal data when visitors use the website.

Last updated: 25 May 2026

1. Who is responsible for this website

The website Ashraellen.com is operated as part of the independent creative and research project Ashraellen, associated with Nikolai Kostyshev.

For privacy-related questions, requests or cooperation matters, you may contact the project at: ashraellen.live@gmail.com.

2. What data may be collected

The website may process limited technical and contact data, depending on how you use it:

  • technical data such as browser type, device type, approximate location derived from network data, pages visited and time of visit;
  • analytics data collected through Google Analytics, with IP anonymisation enabled;
  • data voluntarily provided by you if you contact Ashraellen by email or through external services;
  • technical data used by the browser and service worker to support basic site functionality.

The website does not ask visitors to create accounts and does not intentionally collect sensitive personal data.

3. Why data is used

Data may be used for the following purposes:

  • to make the website available and functional;
  • to understand how visitors use the site and improve its structure, language versions and public materials;
  • to respond to messages, grant inquiries, publishing inquiries or cooperation requests;
  • to maintain security, prevent abuse and diagnose technical problems.

4. Legal basis

Where the GDPR applies, personal data may be processed on the following legal bases:

  • legitimate interest — operating, securing and improving the website;
  • consent — where optional analytics, cookies, subscriptions or similar services require consent;
  • contract or pre-contractual communication — when you contact Ashraellen regarding cooperation, publication, grants or services;
  • legal obligation — where applicable law requires data to be retained or disclosed.

5. Analytics and third-party services

Ashraellen.com currently uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage. The analytics script is configured with IP anonymisation.

The website may link to or embed content from third-party platforms such as YouTube, Google Play Books, Amazon, Instagram, Telegram, payment providers or mailing-list services. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies when you interact with them.

6. Cookies and local technologies

The site may use cookies, local storage, service workers or similar technologies for technical operation, language handling, analytics and performance.

If analytics, newsletter tools or embedded media are expanded in the future, a separate cookie notice or consent mechanism should be used where required by law.

7. Newsletter and updates

If a subscription or update notification service is added to the website, the service may collect your email address and related subscription data. Such data will be used only to send requested updates and related project information, unless you clearly agree to something else.

8. Purchases and external platforms

Books, digital products or paid services may be offered through external platforms such as Amazon, Google Play Books, Stripe or similar providers. Payment and account data are processed by those external platforms under their own terms and privacy policies.

9. How long data is kept

Technical and analytics data are kept only as long as needed for analysis, security and maintenance. Email correspondence may be kept as long as necessary to handle the request and maintain a reasonable record of cooperation or legal obligations.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, portability, objection to processing, and withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.

If you are in Poland or the European Union, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority, including the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website changes, especially if new forms, analytics tools, newsletter systems, payment services or embedded media are added.

This policy is written for transparency and practical orientation. It is not a substitute for individual legal advice.
