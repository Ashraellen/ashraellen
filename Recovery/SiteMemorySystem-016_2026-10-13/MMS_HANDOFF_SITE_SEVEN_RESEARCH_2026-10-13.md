## 5. SEVEN conceptual framing — MUST PRESERVE

SEVEN is NOT:
- a diagnostic system;
- a claim that seven natural psychological mechanisms have been proven;
- a theological verdict;
- an established scientific taxonomy;
- a finished empirical model.

SEVEN asks whether seven historically persistent categories are useful and distinguishable:
- pride
- greed
- lust
- envy
- gluttony
- wrath
- sloth / acedia

Core epistemic rule:
A negative result is valid. If the seven-part scheme proves wrong, that is a result, not a failure.

Literary line:
- GREED is the first active research-through-fiction line.
- Fiction generates cases, questions, counterexamples and stress tests.
- Fiction is not empirical proof.

A central formulation used publicly:
“Seven human vulnerabilities — or seven old names for something more complex?”
